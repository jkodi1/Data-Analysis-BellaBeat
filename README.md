## 📋 Google's 6 Phase Framework
### 1. Ask
* **Business Task:** Analyze smart device usage data to identify trends that can help Bellabeat improve its marketing strategy and product features.
* **Stakeholders:** Bellabeat executive team and marketing team


### 2. Prepare
* Data sourced from Kaggle by Möbius.
* The data is from 30+ Fitbit users who consented to share personal tracker data.
* **Limitations:** Small sample size and significant amount of missing data.

### 3. Process
* Cleaned data for inconsistencies (missing values, duplicate entries).
* Merged multiple datasets (Daily Activity, Sleep, and Calories) to create a unified view of user behavior.

### 4. Analyze & 5. Share
* **Sedentary Time:** Users spend an average of nearly 16 hours/day sedentary. This presents an opportunity for "movement reminders."
* **Sleep Trends:** Most users sleep between 7–8 hours, but there is a negative correlation between sedentary time and sleep quality.
* **Peak Activity:** Data shows peaks in activity between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM (post-work hours).
* **Weekend vs. Weekday:** Users take more steps on Saturdays but spend the most time "resting" or sleeping on Sundays.

### 6. Act
* **In-App Notifications:** Suggested "Reminders to Move" during peak sedentary hours.
* **Personalized Sleep Tips:** Use the Bellabeat app to provide tips for users showing high sedentary minutes to improve sleep quality.
* **Marketing Focus:** Highlight the "Leaf" or "Time" products as tools for work-life balance, specifically targeting the post-work activity window.
